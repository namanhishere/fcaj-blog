# Summary Report: "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week"

### Event Objectives

- Connect young technologists with AWS leaders and industry experts in Agentic AI
- Encourage participants to challenge traditional ways of working using AI architectures
- Provide a platform for teams to build and pitch Agentic AI products within 24 hours
- Showcase real-world applications of AWS Bedrock, AI agents, and agentic workflows
- Foster collaboration, rapid prototyping, and entrepreneurial problem-solving

### Speakers

- **AWS Leaders** – Opening remarks on the future of AI and agentic architectures
- **Hackathon Judges** – Industry experts evaluating projects on business impact and technical execution

### Key Highlights

#### One Team: KFC Conversational Ordering (1st Prize)

- Traditional fast-food apps have high friction — users must download an app, create accounts, and navigate clunky menus
- Built a multi-channel conversational chatbot on Zalo and WhatsApp to place food orders naturally within chat apps
- Leveraged AWS Bedrock and an AI agent with memory to process natural language, handle follow-ups, and finalize orders without users leaving the chat interface

#### Signal Scout: Corporate Intelligence (2nd Prize)

- Gathering business intelligence on competitors' corporate structures and forecasting their success is highly manual and fragmented
- Developed an AI tool for HR and business strategists using web scraping (Tiny Fish) and AWS Bedrock agents
- Collects fragmented competitor data (financial reports, company structure changes) and synthesizes it, then forecasts ROI and risks of adapting a competitor's structural changes

#### Team Plan: AI Cloud Solution Architect

- Cloud Solution Architects often get rushed requests from clients to design architectures and estimate costs within days or hours
- Created an AI-native app where users input natural language requirements and upload company policies
- The AI agent automatically draws AWS architecture diagrams, generates cost estimates, and writes Terraform (Infrastructure as Code) scripts for instant infrastructure deployment

#### Team 3K: "Sheper" Crowd Monitoring

- Congestion and bottlenecks in large public spaces like airports and supermarkets cause operational inefficiencies
- Built a real-time computer vision system using YOLO object detection, AWS Kinesis, and Bedrock agents
- Monitors live camera feeds to detect crowd density in specific zones, with an AI agent acting as a co-pilot for human operators — alerting them to bottlenecks and suggesting staff deployments

#### Six Pillar: Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Workflow

- Financial institutions deal with massive numbers of false-positive alerts for money laundering, forcing analysts to spend hours reviewing valid transactions
- Developed the Adaptive Workflow Engine using a machine-learning model for fast initial detection, followed by three specialized AI sub-agents for KYC profile checks, money flow tracing, and sanctions list verification
- Synthesizes an evidence report and makes an initial decision, escalating only the most ambiguous cases to a human analyst

### Key Takeaways

#### Agentic AI Architectures

- Understood how AI agents with memory can maintain conversational context for complex multi-turn interactions
- Learned how specialized sub-agents (KYC, money tracing, sanctions) can collaborate to solve complex investigative tasks
- Gained insight into how AI agents act as co-pilots rather than autonomous decision-makers in high-stakes scenarios

#### Problem-First Mindset

- The most successful projects focused on a clear, real-world pain point rather than complex technology
- Keeping scope small and focused on an MVP was critical for delivery within 24 hours
- Judges valued business impact and problem understanding over technical complexity

#### Teamwork and Rapid Prototyping

- Effective teams divided tasks efficiently across frontend, backend, AI, and pitching roles
- Putting ego aside and supporting each other through time pressure was essential
- Rapid prototyping with AWS Bedrock and agentic tools significantly accelerated development

### Applying to Work

- Explore AWS Bedrock agents for building conversational and investigative AI workflows
- Apply the sub-agent collaboration pattern for complex, multi-step business processes
- Use YOLO and AWS Kinesis for real-time computer vision monitoring scenarios
- Adopt Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) generation patterns via AI to accelerate cloud deployments
- Integrate a "problem-first" approach in future projects: validate the pain point before designing the solution

### Event Experience

Participating in the **FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week** was an intense and rewarding experience that brought together ambitious young technologists under one roof to build agentic AI products in 24 hours. The hackathon not only challenged our technical skills but also pushed us to think like entrepreneurs.

#### Learning from AWS leaders and judges
- AWS leaders set the tone by emphasizing that AI is not just about automating tasks but about reimagining how we work and solve problems
- Judges provided sharp, constructive feedback that helped teams understand what makes a product truly investable

#### Hands-on exposure to Agentic AI
- Observed five very different applications of agentic AI — from fast-food ordering to anti-money laundering
- Gained practical understanding of how AWS Bedrock, AI agents with memory, and sub-agent orchestration work in production-style prototypes
- Learned how computer vision (YOLO) and streaming data (Kinesis) integrate with AI agents for real-time decision support

#### Collaboration under pressure
- Experienced the dynamics of a 24-hour build sprint — dividing roles, managing fatigue, and iterating rapidly
- Learned that a well-pitched demo with a clear problem statement can outperform technically complex but poorly communicated solutions

#### Lessons learned
- Agentic AI is most powerful when it augments human decision-making rather than replacing it entirely
- Keeping scope lean (MVP) and focusing on the user's pain point is always the winning strategy
- Team chemistry and effective role division matter as much as technical competence in high-pressure environments
